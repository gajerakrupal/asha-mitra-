assignment1. Pregnancy registration and check-ups

assignmentRegister early

Register every pregnant woman as soon as pregnancy is confirmed, within the first 12 weeks. Fill the MCP (Mother and Child Protection) card and give it to her. She must bring this card to every visit.

lightbulb
Remember

Early registration = early care. Visit newly married women and ask about missed periods.

calendar_monthAt least 4 check-ups

Every pregnant woman needs at least 4 ante-natal check-ups (ANC). The first in the first 3 months. Also motivate her to attend the PMSMA check-up by a doctor on the 9th of every month in the 2nd and 3rd trimester.

lightbulb
Remember

Keep a list of all pregnant women in your area and their next check-up date.

monitor_heartWhat is checked at ANC

Weight, blood pressure, haemoglobin (Hb), urine test, blood sugar, belly check for baby's growth, and blood tests for HIV and syphilis. Hb below 11 g/dl means anaemia.

lightbulb
Remember

BP 140/90 or more is high. Refer her to the doctor.

medicationTablets and injections

Folic acid in the first 3 months. From the 4th month: 1 IFA (iron) tablet daily for at least 180 days and 2 calcium tablets daily. One albendazole (deworming) tablet in the 2nd trimester. Td injection: 2 doses (or 1 booster if given in the last 3 years).

lightbulb
Remember

Take iron and calcium at different times. Iron tablet with lemon water is better absorbed; not with tea.

restaurantFood and rest

She should eat one extra meal every day: green leafy vegetables, pulses, milk, eggs or meat if she eats them, and fruits. She needs 2 hours of rest in the day and 8 hours of sleep at night. No tobacco or alcohol.

lightbulb
Remember

Talk to the whole family, not only to the woman.

warning2. Danger signs in pregnancy

bloodtypeBleeding

Any bleeding from the vagina during pregnancy is dangerous. Send her to the hospital at once.

lightbulb
Remember

Call 108 or 102 for free ambulance.

psychologyHeadache, blurred vision, fits

Severe headache, blurred vision, swelling of face and hands, or convulsions (fits) are signs of high BP in pregnancy (pre-eclampsia / eclampsia). This can kill the mother and baby.

lightbulb
Remember

Do not wait. Refer immediately to a facility with a doctor.

thermostatFever, breathlessness, paleness

High fever, feeling breathless, very pale eyes, tongue and nails (severe anaemia) need urgent care.

lightbulb
Remember

Severe anaemia (Hb below 7) needs treatment at the hospital.

child_friendlyBaby moves less, water leaks

If the baby's movements reduce or stop, or water leaks from the vagina before labour pains, she must go to the hospital at once. Labour lasting more than 12 hours is also dangerous.

lightbulb
Remember

Teach every family these danger signs at the first visit.

local_hospital3. Birth preparedness and safe delivery

checklistMake a birth plan

Decide in advance: which hospital, how to reach there (108/102 number), who will go with her, money for emergency, and a blood donor from the family.

lightbulb
Remember

Write the phone numbers on the MCP card.

local_hospitalDelivery only in hospital

Every delivery should happen in a health facility with trained staff. Under JSSK, delivery, medicines, tests, food, blood and transport are free in government hospitals. Under JSY, the mother gets cash help.

lightbulb
Remember

Home delivery increases the risk of death for the mother and baby.

bedStay 48 hours

After a normal delivery, the mother and baby should stay in the hospital for at least 48 hours. Most dangers like heavy bleeding happen in the first 2 days.

lightbulb
Remember

Visit the mother at home on the day she returns.

warningDanger signs after delivery

Heavy bleeding, fever, foul-smelling discharge, severe belly pain, fits, or feeling very sad and not caring for the baby. Refer her at once.

lightbulb
Remember

More than 1 pad soaked in 5 minutes means heavy bleeding.

child_care4. Home-based newborn care (HBNC)

calendar_monthHBNC home visits

Visit the newborn at home on day 3, 7, 14, 21, 28 and 42 after a hospital delivery. If the baby was born at home, also visit on day 1. Visit low birth weight babies more often.

lightbulb
Remember

Fill the HBNC format at every visit.

favoriteBreastfeed within 1 hour

Start breastfeeding within 1 hour of birth. The first yellow milk (colostrum) is the baby's first vaccine. Give only breast milk for 6 months - no water, honey, ghutti or other milk.

lightbulb
Remember

Feed 8-12 times in 24 hours, day and night.

homeKeep the baby warm

Dry the baby, wrap in clean cloth, cover the head, and keep with the mother. Skin-to-skin contact (Kangaroo Mother Care) is best, especially for small babies. Delay the first bath.

lightbulb
Remember

Keep the cord clean and dry. Do not apply anything on it.

scaleWeigh the baby

Weigh every newborn. A baby weighing less than 2.5 kg is low birth weight and needs extra care: more frequent feeding, KMC, and more visits.

lightbulb
Remember

Babies below 1.8 kg or unable to suck should be referred.

warningNewborn danger signs

Not able to suck, fits, fast breathing (60 or more per minute), chest in-drawing, very cold or hot body, baby very sleepy or limp, yellow palms and soles, or red and pus-filled cord. Refer at once.

lightbulb
Remember

Count breathing for a full 1 minute when the baby is calm.

family_restroom5. Family planning

family_restroomWhy spacing matters

A gap of at least 3 years between two children keeps the mother and child healthy. The first child should come after the age of 20.

lightbulb
Remember

Talk to the couple together when possible.

medicationSpacing methods

Condoms, daily pills (Mala-N), weekly pill Chhaya, the Antara injection every 3 months, and Copper-T (IUCD) - which can also be put right after delivery (PPIUCD).

lightbulb
Remember

You can give condoms, pills and pregnancy test kits from your drug kit.

health_and_safetyPermanent methods

For couples who do not want more children: sterilisation for women and no-scalpel vasectomy (NSV) for men. NSV is simple and quick. Both are free, and the person receives compensation.

lightbulb
Remember

Clear myths: NSV does not cause weakness in men.

Register within the first 12 weeks (first trimester) so care starts early.

  2. 1 daily for at least 180 days
  3. 10 tablets in total
  4. Only in the 9th month

Answer: 1 daily for at least 180 days

From the 4th month, 1 IFA tablet daily for at least 180 days.

  1. Give a pain tablet and wait
  2. Tell her to rest at home
  3. Refer her to the hospital immediately
  4. Visit again next week
Show answer
Answer: Refer her to the hospital immediately

These are signs of pre-eclampsia. It is an emergency.

  1. After 3 days
  2. After the first bath
  3. Within 1 hour of birth
  4. After giving honey

Answer: Within 1 hour of birth

Start within 1 hour. Colostrum protects the baby.

  1. Normal
  2. Fast breathing - a danger sign

  4. Normal for a sleeping baby

Answer: Fast breathing - a danger sign

60 or more breaths per minute in a newborn is fast breathing. Refer.
