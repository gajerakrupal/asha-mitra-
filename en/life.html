1. Which salt should families use?
  1. Any salt
  2. Iodised salt
  3. Rock salt only
  4. No salt
Show answer
Answer: Iodised salt

Iodised salt protects brain development and prevents goitre.

2. How long should proper handwashing with soap take?
  1. 5 seconds
  2. About 40 seconds
  3. 5 minutes
  4. Only a quick rinse
Show answer
Answer: About 40 seconds

About 40 seconds, covering all parts of the hands.

3. Which of these tobacco products is safe?
  1. Gutkha
  2. Khaini
  3. Bidi
  4. None of them
Show answer
Answer: None of them

No tobacco product is safe.

4. What is the Quit Tobacco helpline number?
  1. 108

1800-11-2356 is the free Quit Tobacco helpline.

5. How much physical activity should adults do?
  1. 30 minutes on at least 5 days a week
  2. 10 minutes once a week
  3. Only on holidays
  4. None needed after 40
Show answer
Answer: 30 minutes on at least 5 days a week

At least 30 minutes on 5 or more days a week.
