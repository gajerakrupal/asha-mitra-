restaurant1. Balanced diet

restaurantA healthy plate

Every meal should have: cereals (rice, roti, millets like bajra and jowar), pulses or beans, vegetables and fruits, and milk or curd. Add eggs, fish or meat if the family eats them. Use little oil, sugar and salt.

lightbulb
Remember

The more colours on the plate, the more nutrients.

scienceIodised salt

Use only iodised salt. Iodine protects a child's brain development and prevents goitre. Store salt in a closed container away from heat and damp.

lightbulb
Remember

Check the packet for 'Iodised' written on it.

child_careAnganwadi food (POSHAN)

Under POSHAN Abhiyaan, pregnant women, breastfeeding mothers and children 6 months to 6 years get take-home ration or hot cooked meals from the Anganwadi. Make sure every eligible family is registered.

lightbulb
Remember

Take-home ration is for the mother or child, not for the whole family.

clean_hands2. Toilets and hygiene

homeUse a toilet always

Every family member should use a toilet, every time. Open defecation spreads diarrhoea, worms and typhoid. Children's stool should also be put in the toilet. Keep the toilet clean and use water after use.

lightbulb
Remember

Help families apply for toilet support under Swachh Bharat Mission.

clean_handsHandwashing steps

Wet hands, apply soap, rub palms, back of hands, between fingers, backs of fingers, thumbs and fingertips, then wrists. Rinse and dry with a clean cloth. It should take about 40 seconds.

lightbulb
Remember

Wash hands before eating, before cooking, before feeding a child, and after toilet.

waterClean surroundings

Do not let water collect near the house — it breeds mosquitoes. Put garbage in a pit or dustbin, keep wet and dry waste separate, and keep animals' shelters away from the kitchen.

lightbulb
Remember

Plan a village cleaning day with the VHSNC.

smoke_free3. Tobacco and alcohol

smoke_freeTobacco harms

All tobacco — bidi, cigarette, gutkha, mawa, khaini, snuff — causes cancer, heart attack, stroke, TB and lung disease. Smokeless tobacco is not safe. Smoke also harms children and pregnant women at home.

lightbulb
Remember

Quit Tobacco helpline: 1800-11-2356 (free).

campaignHelp people quit — 5 A's

Ask every adult about tobacco use. Advise them clearly to quit. Assess if they are ready. Assist — help them set a quit date and handle cravings. Arrange follow-up and refer to the tobacco cessation centre.

lightbulb
Remember

Most people need several tries to quit. Keep encouraging.

infoTobacco law and alcohol

By law (COTPA), smoking in public places is banned and tobacco cannot be sold to people under 18 or within 100 yards of a school. Alcohol harms the liver, heart and family life, and leads to accidents and violence. Refer people who want to stop drinking to the MO.

lightbulb
Remember

Tele-MANAS 14416 can also help with addiction.

directions_walk4. Physical activity and healthy weight

directions_walkMove every day

Adults should do at least 30 minutes of brisk walking, cycling, yoga or other activity on at least 5 days a week. Children should play actively for at least 1 hour every day.

lightbulb
Remember

Walking together in a group makes it easier to continue.

scaleWaist size and risk

A waist of more than 80 cm in women or more than 90 cm in men increases the risk of diabetes, high BP and heart disease. Measure the waist at the level of the navel while filling CBAC.

lightbulb
Remember

Losing even a little weight lowers the risk.

wb_sunnySleep and stress

Adults need 7 to 8 hours of sleep. Less screen time, time with family, yoga and breathing exercises reduce stress. Too much mobile use is harmful for children's eyes, sleep and studies.

lightbulb
Remember

Set a no-mobile time at meals and before bed.
