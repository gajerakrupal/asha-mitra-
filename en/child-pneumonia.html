Pneumonia in children

airCount the breathing

If a child has cough or difficulty in breathing, count breaths for 1 full minute. Fast breathing is: 60 or more (under 2 months), 50 or more (2-12 months), 40 or more (1-5 years).

lightbulb
Remember

Count when the child is calm or sleeping.

warningChest in-drawing and danger signs

If the lower chest goes inside when the child breathes in, it is severe pneumonia. Also danger: not able to drink, fits, very sleepy, or vomiting everything.

lightbulb
Remember

Refer at once. Keep the child warm and continue breastfeeding on the way.

local_hospitalTreatment and prevention

Fast breathing needs antibiotic treatment from the ANM or doctor, as per your state's guideline. Prevent pneumonia with breastfeeding, full immunization (including PCV), and a smoke-free home.

lightbulb
Remember

Cooking smoke is harmful. Keep children away from the chulha.
