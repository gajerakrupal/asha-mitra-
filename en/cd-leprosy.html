Communicable Diseases · 2/4

Leprosy

healingSigns of leprosy

A pale or reddish patch on the skin with loss of feeling (no sensation of touch, pain or heat). Also thick shiny skin, numbness or weakness in hands and feet.

lightbulb
Remember

Touch the patch gently with cotton to check feeling.

medicationFree cure with MDT

Leprosy is curable with Multi-Drug Therapy (MDT), free at government facilities. Early treatment prevents disability. After the first dose, the patient does not spread infection.

lightbulb
Remember

Leprosy is not caused by sin or a curse. Remove stigma.

homeSelf-care

Patients with numb hands or feet should check them daily for wounds, soak in water and oil the skin, and wear protective footwear.

lightbulb
Remember

Refer any new weakness, pain in nerves or eye redness.
