groups1. Adolescent health (RKSK)

groupsWho are adolescents

Boys and girls aged 10 to 19 years are adolescents. Their body and mind change fast. They need correct information on nutrition, body changes, mental health, and staying away from tobacco, alcohol and drugs.

lightbulb
Remember

Talk to adolescents with respect and in private. Do not scold or shame them.

local_hospitalAdolescent Friendly Health Clinic

Under RKSK (Rashtriya Kishor Swasthya Karyakram), adolescents can get free counselling and treatment at Adolescent Friendly Health Clinics (AFHC) at PHC, CHC and district hospitals. Everything they share is kept confidential.

lightbulb
Remember

Tell adolescents the day and place of the clinic nearest to them.

volunteer_activismPeer educators

In each village, peer educators (Saathiya) — 2 boys and 2 girls — are chosen to share health messages with other adolescents. Help them organise the Adolescent Health and Wellness Day in your village.

lightbulb
Remember

Adolescents often listen to friends more than to adults.

water_drop2. Periods and menstrual hygiene

calendar_monthPeriods are normal

Periods (menstruation) usually start between 10 and 15 years of age. A normal cycle comes every 21 to 35 days and bleeding lasts 2 to 7 days. It is a normal, healthy body process, not an illness or something dirty.

lightbulb
Remember

Talk to girls about periods before they start, so they are not scared.

clean_handsStaying clean

Use a clean sanitary pad or a clean cotton cloth. Change it every 4 to 6 hours. Wash reusable cloth with soap and dry it in sunlight. Wrap used pads in paper and put them in a dustbin, or burn or bury them. Wash hands before and after changing.

lightbulb
Remember

Drying cloth in the sun kills germs. Do not hide it in a dark, damp place.

lightbulbBreak the myths

During periods a girl can bathe, go to school, play, cook and eat all normal foods, including sour foods. There is no need to keep her separate from the family.

lightbulb
Remember

Talk to mothers too — they decide many home rules.

warningWhen to refer

Refer to the doctor if: bleeding is very heavy (a pad soaked in 1 to 2 hours), bleeding lasts more than 7 days, very severe pain, no period for 3 months (and not pregnant), or no periods at all by 15 years of age.

lightbulb
Remember

Heavy periods can cause anaemia. Check her Hb.

bloodtype3. Anaemia Mukt Bharat

sickWhat is anaemia

Anaemia means low haemoglobin (Hb) in the blood. Signs: tiredness, weakness, breathlessness, dizziness, and pale eyes, tongue and nails. It reduces a child's ability to study and increases risk to mothers in pregnancy.

lightbulb
Remember

Hb below 12 g/dl in adolescent girls and non-pregnant women is anaemia; below 11 g/dl in pregnant women.

medicationWeekly iron tablet (WIFS)

Adolescents 10 to 19 years: 1 blue IFA tablet every week, the whole year, given in school or by you at the Anganwadi for out-of-school adolescents. Women of reproductive age (not pregnant or breastfeeding): 1 red IFA tablet every week. Deworming with albendazole twice a year (National Deworming Day).

lightbulb
Remember

Take the iron tablet after food to reduce stomach upset. Black stool is normal.

restaurantIron-rich food

Eat green leafy vegetables, pulses, sprouts, jaggery, dates, eggs, meat or fish. Eat vitamin C fruits like amla, guava or lemon with meals — it helps the body take up iron. Do not drink tea or coffee within 1 hour of meals.

lightbulb
Remember

Cooking in an iron kadai adds iron to food.

scienceTest, Treat, Talk (T3)

Help people get their Hb tested at T3 camps and at the health centre. People with anaemia are given treatment doses of iron. Severe anaemia (Hb below 7 g/dl) must be referred to the hospital.

lightbulb
Remember

Keep a list of anaemic girls and women and follow them up every month.

family_restroom4. Right age for marriage and pregnancy

infoLegal age of marriage

By law, a girl must be at least 18 years old and a boy at least 21 years old to marry. Early marriage harms a girl's health and education.

lightbulb
Remember

If you hear of a child marriage, inform the ANM, the Anganwadi worker or call Childline 1098.

pregnant_womanFirst pregnancy after 20

Pregnancy before 20 years of age is risky: more anaemia, high BP, difficult delivery, and low birth weight babies. Newly married couples can delay the first pregnancy using condoms or pills.

lightbulb
Remember

Give newly married couples the Nayi Pahal kit and explain the methods.

psychologyMental health of adolescents

Watch for adolescents who stay sad, stop going to school, stop eating, fight a lot, or start using tobacco, alcohol or drugs. Listen to them without judging and connect them to the AFHC counsellor or Tele-MANAS 14416.

lightbulb
Remember

Exam stress and family problems are common. A kind word helps.

1. Adolescents are people in which age group?
  1. 5 to 9 years
  2. 10 to 19 years
  3. 20 to 24 years
  4. Only 13 to 15 years
Show answer
Answer: 10 to 19 years

Adolescents are 10 to 19 years old.

2. How often should a sanitary pad or cloth be changed?
  1. Once a day
  2. Every 4 to 6 hours
  3. Only when fully soaked
  4. Every 2 days
Show answer
Answer: Every 4 to 6 hours

Change every 4 to 6 hours to stay clean and prevent infection.

3. Under WIFS, how often does an adolescent take the blue IFA tablet?
  1. Every day
  2. Once a week
  3. Once a month
  4. Only when sick
Show answer
Answer: Once a week

One blue IFA tablet every week, throughout the year.

4. Which drink should NOT be taken with meals because it reduces iron absorption?
  1. Lemon water
  2. Tea
  3. Water
  4. Buttermilk
Show answer
Answer: Tea

Tea and coffee reduce iron absorption. Avoid them for 1 hour around meals.

5. What is the legal minimum age of marriage for a girl in India?
  1. 16 years
  2. 18 years
  3. 21 years
  4. 14 years
Show answer
Answer: 18 years

Girls: 18 years. Boys: 21 years.

6. A 15-year-old girl says her period soaks a pad every hour. You should:
  1. Tell her it is normal
  2. Refer her to the doctor and check Hb
  3. Tell her to eat less
  4. Wait for 6 months
Show answer
Answer: Refer her to the doctor and check Hb

Very heavy bleeding needs a doctor and can cause anaemia.
