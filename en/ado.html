Quiz

Try these questions. Tap "Show answer" to check.

1. Adolescents are people in which age group?
  1. 5 to 9 years
  2. 10 to 19 years
  3. 20 to 24 years
  4. Only 13 to 15 years
Show answer
Answer: 10 to 19 years

Adolescents are 10 to 19 years old.

2. How often should a sanitary pad or cloth be changed?
  1. Once a day
  2. Every 4 to 6 hours
  3. Only when fully soaked
  4. Every 2 days
Show answer
Answer: Every 4 to 6 hours

Change every 4 to 6 hours to stay clean and prevent infection.

3. Under WIFS, how often does an adolescent take the blue IFA tablet?
  1. Every day
  2. Once a week
  3. Once a month
  4. Only when sick
Show answer
Answer: Once a week

One blue IFA tablet every week, throughout the year.

4. Which drink should NOT be taken with meals because it reduces iron absorption?
  1. Lemon water
  2. Tea
  3. Water
  4. Buttermilk
Show answer
Answer: Tea

Tea and coffee reduce iron absorption. Avoid them for 1 hour around meals.

5. What is the legal minimum age of marriage for a girl in India?
  1. 16 years
  2. 18 years
  3. 21 years
  4. 14 years
Show answer
Answer: 18 years

Girls: 18 years. Boys: 21 years.

6. A 15-year-old girl says her period soaks a pad every hour. You should:
  1. Tell her it is normal
  2. Refer her to the doctor and check Hb
  3. Tell her to eat less
  4. Wait for 6 months
Show answer
Answer: Refer her to the doctor and check Hb

Very heavy bleeding needs a doctor and can cause anaemia.
