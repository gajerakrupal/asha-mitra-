Vector-borne Diseases (NVBDCP) · 1/5

Malaria

bug_reportHow malaria spreads

Malaria spreads by the bite of the female Anopheles mosquito, which bites at night. Signs: fever with chills and shivering, sweating, headache and body ache. Fever may come on alternate days.

lightbulb
Remember

In a malaria area, think of malaria in every fever.

scienceTest every fever

Test every fever case with a Rapid Diagnostic Test (RDT) and also make a blood slide, as per your training. Test within 24 hours of fever. Record in the fever register.

lightbulb
Remember

Check the expiry date of RDT kits.

medicationTreat fully

Give treatment as per the age-wise drug chart in your kit. P. vivax: chloroquine for 3 days + primaquine for 14 days. P. falciparum: ACT (artesunate combination) + single dose primaquine. The full course must be completed.

lightbulb
Remember

Do not give primaquine to pregnant women or infants. Refer pregnant women with malaria to the MO.

warningSevere malaria

Refer at once if there is unconsciousness, fits, repeated vomiting, cannot eat or drink, very pale, yellow eyes, dark urine, or very little urine.

lightbulb
Remember

Severe malaria can kill within hours.
