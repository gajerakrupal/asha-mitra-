bug_report1. Malaria

bug_reportHow malaria spreads

Malaria spreads by the bite of the female Anopheles mosquito, which bites at night. Signs: fever with chills and shivering, sweating, headache and body ache. Fever may come on alternate days.

lightbulb
Remember

In a malaria area, think of malaria in every fever.

scienceTest every fever

Test every fever case with a Rapid Diagnostic Test (RDT) and also make a blood slide, as per your training. Test within 24 hours of fever. Record in the fever register.

lightbulb
Remember

Check the expiry date of RDT kits.

medicationTreat fully

Give treatment as per the age-wise drug chart in your kit. P. vivax: chloroquine for 3 days + primaquine for 14 days. P. falciparum: ACT (artesunate combination) + single dose primaquine. The full course must be completed.

lightbulb
Remember

Do not give primaquine to pregnant women or infants. Refer pregnant women with malaria to the MO.

warningSevere malaria

Refer at once if there is unconsciousness, fits, repeated vomiting, cannot eat or drink, very pale, yellow eyes, dark urine, or very little urine.

lightbulb
Remember

Severe malaria can kill within hours.

thermostat2. Dengue and chikungunya

bug_reportAedes mosquito

Dengue and chikungunya spread by the Aedes mosquito. It has white stripes, bites in the daytime, and breeds in clean stored water: coolers, tanks, tyres, pots, and flower vases.

lightbulb
Remember

One spoon of water is enough for mosquito eggs.

thermostatSigns

Dengue: sudden high fever, severe headache, pain behind the eyes, body and joint pain, rash. Chikungunya: fever with severe joint pain that may last for weeks.

lightbulb
Remember

Send for the dengue test at the health centre.

local_drinkHome care

Plenty of fluids - ORS, water, coconut water, juice. Rest. Give only paracetamol for fever. Do not give aspirin, ibuprofen or other pain killers - they can cause bleeding.

lightbulb
Remember

Keep the patient under a mosquito net to stop spread.

warningWarning signs - refer

Severe belly pain, repeated vomiting, bleeding from gums or nose, black stools, cold and clammy skin, restlessness or drowsiness. These usually come as the fever goes down (day 3 to 7).

lightbulb
Remember

Fever going down does not always mean the patient is safe.

pest_control3. Mosquito control in the village

deleteStop breeding

Empty, scrub and dry coolers, tanks and pots once a week ('Dry Day'). Cover all water containers. Throw away old tyres, coconut shells and broken pots, or keep them upside down.

lightbulb
Remember

Do a house-to-house check with families - show them larvae.

bedSleep under nets

Everyone, especially pregnant women and children, should sleep under long-lasting insecticidal nets (LLIN) every night. Do not wash the net often. Tuck it under the mattress.

lightbulb
Remember

Use full sleeves, and repellents where available.

homeSpray and fish

Encourage families to allow indoor residual spraying (IRS) and not to plaster or paint walls after spray. Larva-eating fish (Gambusia, guppy) can be put in ponds, wells and tanks.

lightbulb
Remember

Inform the ANM about new breeding sites.

accessibility4. Lymphatic filariasis (hathipaon)

accessibilityWhat is filaria

Filaria spreads by the Culex mosquito, which breeds in dirty water. The worm damages the body over years and causes swelling of legs (hathipaon) or hydrocele. Most infected people have no signs for years.

lightbulb
Remember

It can be prevented but swelling cannot be fully cured.

medicationMass drug administration (MDA)

During MDA, everyone eligible must take the tablets (DEC + albendazole, or with ivermectin - IDA) in front of the health worker. Not given to children under 2 years, pregnant women, and seriously ill people.

lightbulb
Remember

Tell people to eat before taking the tablets.

clean_handsCare of swollen limbs

Wash the swollen leg daily with soap and water, dry it well, keep the skin clean, exercise and raise the leg. Hydrocele can be cured by a free operation.

lightbulb
Remember

Make a line list of patients with swelling or hydrocele.

sick5. Japanese Encephalitis and Kala-azar

psychologyJapanese Encephalitis (JE / AES)

JE spreads by the Culex mosquito from pigs and birds, in rice-growing areas. A child with fever and confusion, unconsciousness or fits needs urgent referral. JE vaccine is given in affected districts at 9 months and 16-24 months.

lightbulb
Remember

Every minute matters in fever with fits.

bug_reportKala-azar

Kala-azar spreads by the sandfly in some areas of Bihar, Jharkhand, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal. Signs: fever for more than 2 weeks, big spleen (swelling in the left belly), weight loss, darkening of skin.

lightbulb
Remember

Test (rK39) and treatment are free. Sandflies breed in cracks of mud walls - plaster them.

2. In a malaria area, a person has fever. What is the first step?
  1. Give paracetamol and wait 1 week
  2. Test with RDT/blood slide
  3. Give antibiotics
  4. Refer only if fever lasts 1 month

Answer: Test with RDT/blood slide

Test every fever within 24 hours.

3. Which medicine can be given for fever in dengue?
  1. Aspirin
  2. Ibuprofen
  3. Paracetamol
  4. Any pain killer
Show answer
Answer: Paracetamol

Only paracetamol. Aspirin and ibuprofen can cause bleeding.

4. How often should coolers and water tanks be emptied and dried?
  1. Once a year
  2. Once a week
  3. Once a month
  4. Never
Show answer
Answer: Once a week

Once a week - Aedes eggs become mosquitoes in about 7 days.

  3. Pregnant women
  4. Elderly healthy people

Answer: Pregnant women

Not for children under 2, pregnant women, and seriously ill people.

6. Primaquine should not be given to:
  1. Adult men

Primaquine is not given to pregnant women and infants.
