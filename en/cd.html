masks1. Tuberculosis (TB)

sickSigns of TB

Cough for 2 weeks or more, fever especially in the evening, weight loss, night sweats, loss of appetite, or blood in sputum. TB can also affect glands, bones and other parts of the body.

lightbulb
Remember

Any one of these signs = presumptive TB. Send for testing.

scienceTesting is free

Help the person give a sputum sample for testing (microscopy or NAAT) at the health centre. Chest X-ray may also be done. Testing and treatment are free in government facilities.

lightbulb
Remember

Collect the sample in the early morning.

medicationComplete the treatment

TB is fully curable. Treatment lasts at least 6 months. The patient must not stop medicines when feeling better. As a treatment supporter, make sure the patient takes medicines daily.

lightbulb
Remember

Stopping treatment early can cause drug-resistant TB.

volunteer_activismSupport for patients

Every TB patient gets monthly nutrition support under Nikshay Poshan Yojana (to the bank account). Screen family members and close contacts; they may get TB preventive treatment.

lightbulb
Remember

Help link the patient's bank account on Ni-kshay.

healing2. Leprosy

healingSigns of leprosy

A pale or reddish patch on the skin with loss of feeling (no sensation of touch, pain or heat). Also thick shiny skin, numbness or weakness in hands and feet.

lightbulb
Remember

Touch the patch gently with cotton to check feeling.

medicationFree cure with MDT

Leprosy is curable with Multi-Drug Therapy (MDT), free at government facilities. Early treatment prevents disability. After the first dose, the patient does not spread infection.

lightbulb
Remember

Leprosy is not caused by sin or a curse. Remove stigma.

homeSelf-care

Patients with numb hands or feet should check them daily for wounds, soak in water and oil the skin, and wear protective footwear.

lightbulb
Remember

Refer any new weakness, pain in nerves or eye redness.

pets3. Dog bite and rabies

petsRabies kills - but can be prevented

Rabies spreads through the bite, scratch or lick on broken skin by an infected dog, cat, monkey or other animal. Once symptoms start, it is almost always fatal. Correct first aid and vaccine prevent it fully.

lightbulb
Remember

Never ignore any animal bite, even a small scratch.

clean_handsWash the wound at once

Wash the wound with soap and plenty of running water for 15 minutes. Then apply an antiseptic like povidone-iodine. Do not apply chilli, oil, turmeric or lime. Do not stitch or bandage tightly.

lightbulb
Remember

Washing well removes most of the virus.

vaccinesVaccine the same day

Send the person to the health centre the same day for anti-rabies vaccine (ARV). Complete all doses on the dates given. Deep bites or bites on the head, face or hands also need rabies immunoglobulin (RIG).

lightbulb
Remember

Vaccine is free in government facilities. Remind the person of every due date.

water4. Water-borne diseases and outbreaks

waterSafe drinking water

Diarrhoea, cholera, typhoid and jaundice (hepatitis A and E) spread through dirty water and food. Drink boiled or chlorinated water. Store water covered and take it out with a ladle.

lightbulb
Remember

Put chlorine tablets in water as advised by the ANM.

clean_handsHandwashing

Wash hands with soap and water for 40 seconds: before cooking, before eating, before feeding a child, and after using the toilet or cleaning a child.

lightbulb
Remember

Use a toilet always. No open defecation.

campaignReport unusual cases

If many people in your village get similar illness at the same time - fever, diarrhoea, jaundice, rash - inform the ANM and PHC immediately. This helps stop an outbreak early (IDSP).

lightbulb
Remember

Also report any death from an unknown illness.

1. A man has cough for 3 weeks and weight loss. What will you do?
  1. Give cough syrup
  2. Send for sputum test for TB
  3. Wait for another month
  4. Tell him to rest
Show answer
Answer: Send for sputum test for TB

Cough of 2 weeks or more = presumptive TB. Get tested.

2. How long is TB treatment at least?
  1. 1 week
  2. 1 month
  3. 6 months
  4. Until the cough stops
Show answer
Answer: 6 months

At least 6 months. Do not stop early.

3. A skin patch with loss of feeling may be a sign of:
  1. Allergy
  2. Leprosy
  3. Dengue
  4. Malaria
Show answer
Answer: Leprosy

A patch with loss of sensation is the main sign of leprosy.

4. What is the first aid for a dog bite?
  1. Apply chilli powder
  2. Tie a tight bandage
  3. Wash with soap and running water for 15 minutes
  4. Do nothing if the bite is small
Show answer
Answer: Wash with soap and running water for 15 minutes

Wash for 15 minutes, apply antiseptic, and get the vaccine the same day.

5. Five people in one street get diarrhoea on the same day. You should:
  1. Inform the ANM and PHC immediately
  2. Wait and watch
  3. Tell only the Sarpanch
  4. Give everyone antibiotics
Show answer
Answer: Inform the ANM and PHC immediately

Clusters of cases may be an outbreak. Report early.
