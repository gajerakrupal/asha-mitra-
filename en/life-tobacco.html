Tobacco and alcohol

smoke_freeTobacco harms

All tobacco — bidi, cigarette, gutkha, mawa, khaini, snuff — causes cancer, heart attack, stroke, TB and lung disease. Smokeless tobacco is not safe. Smoke also harms children and pregnant women at home.

lightbulb
Remember

Quit Tobacco helpline: 1800-11-2356 (free).

campaignHelp people quit — 5 A's

Ask every adult about tobacco use. Advise them clearly to quit. Assess if they are ready. Assist — help them set a quit date and handle cravings. Arrange follow-up and refer to the tobacco cessation centre.

lightbulb
Remember

Most people need several tries to quit. Keep encouraging.

infoTobacco law and alcohol

By law (COTPA), smoking in public places is banned and tobacco cannot be sold to people under 18 or within 100 yards of a school. Alcohol harms the liver, heart and family life, and leads to accidents and violence. Refer people who want to stop drinking to the MO.

lightbulb
Remember

Tele-MANAS 14416 can also help with addiction.
