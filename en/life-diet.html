Nutrition, Sanitation & Healthy Habits · 1/4

Balanced diet

restaurantA healthy plate

Every meal should have: cereals (rice, roti, millets like bajra and jowar), pulses or beans, vegetables and fruits, and milk or curd. Add eggs, fish or meat if the family eats them. Use little oil, sugar and salt.

lightbulb
Remember

The more colours on the plate, the more nutrients.

scienceIodised salt

Use only iodised salt. Iodine protects a child's brain development and prevents goitre. Store salt in a closed container away from heat and damp.

lightbulb
Remember

Check the packet for 'Iodised' written on it.

child_careAnganwadi food (POSHAN)

Under POSHAN Abhiyaan, pregnant women, breastfeeding mothers and children 6 months to 6 years get take-home ration or hot cooked meals from the Anganwadi. Make sure every eligible family is registered.

lightbulb
Remember

Take-home ration is for the mother or child, not for the whole family.
