fact_check1. Population enumeration and CBAC

groupsList every family

Make a list of all families and members in your area. Fill the CBAC (Community Based Assessment Checklist) form for every person aged 30 years and above.

lightbulb
Remember

Update the list when there is a birth, death, marriage or migration.

fact_checkCBAC score

CBAC asks about age, tobacco, alcohol, waist size, physical activity, and family history of BP or diabetes. A score of more than 4 means higher risk. It also asks about signs of TB, cancer, and other diseases.

lightbulb
Remember

Everyone above 30 needs screening. Those with score above 4 are a priority.

local_hospitalMobilise for screening

Bring people to the Ayushman Arogya Mandir (Sub-centre / PHC) for screening of BP, diabetes, and oral, breast and cervical cancer.

lightbulb
Remember

Keep a follow-up list of all people on treatment.

monitor_heart2. High BP and diabetes

monitor_heartSilent diseases

High BP and diabetes usually have no signs in the beginning. Over years they damage the heart, brain, kidneys and eyes, and cause heart attack and stroke. Only screening can find them early.

lightbulb
Remember

BP 140/90 or more on check-up - send to the CHO/MO.

medicationMedicines every day

Once started, BP and diabetes medicines must be taken every day, usually for life. Do not stop when feeling well. Get free medicines and monthly check-ups from the Ayushman Arogya Mandir.

lightbulb
Remember

Help patients who run out of medicines to get their refill in time.

directions_walkHealthy lifestyle

Less salt (less than 1 teaspoon a day), less oil, sugar and fried food. More vegetables and fruits. Walk or exercise 30 minutes daily. Stop tobacco and alcohol. Keep weight in control.

lightbulb
Remember

Pickle, papad and namkeen have a lot of salt.

warningEmergency signs

Sudden weakness of face, arm or leg, difficulty speaking (stroke), chest pain spreading to the arm or jaw, sweating (heart attack). Call 108 immediately.

lightbulb
Remember

In stroke, reaching the hospital in the first 4 hours saves lives.

health_and_safety3. Common cancers: oral, breast, cervical

smoke_freeOral cancer

Tobacco (gutkha, khaini, bidi, cigarette) and alcohol cause mouth cancer. Signs: white or red patch in the mouth, ulcer that does not heal in 2 weeks, difficulty opening the mouth.

lightbulb
Remember

Encourage tobacco users to quit. Tobacco quit line: 1800-11-2356.

womanBreast cancer

Signs: lump in breast or armpit, change in shape of breast, discharge or blood from the nipple, skin like orange peel. Women aged 30 and above should get a breast examination.

lightbulb
Remember

A painless lump also needs a check-up.

womanCervical cancer

Signs: bleeding between periods, after sex, or after menopause, and foul-smelling discharge. Women aged 30-65 should get the VIA screening test. HPV vaccine for girls prevents it.

lightbulb
Remember

Cervical cancer is fully curable if found early.

psychology4. Mental health and elderly care

psychologyMental health

Signs to notice: sad for more than 2 weeks, not sleeping or eating, not interested in anything, talking to self, hearing voices, very suspicious, or fits (epilepsy). These are illnesses and can be treated.

lightbulb
Remember

Listen without judging. Mental illness is not caused by spirits or black magic.

phoneGet help

Refer to the MO or the district mental health programme. Free counselling on Tele-MANAS: 14416. If a person talks about ending their life, do not leave them alone and get help urgently.

lightbulb
Remember

Also watch for alcohol and drug problems.

elderlyCare for the elderly

Check elderly people for poor vision (cataract), hearing loss, falls, memory loss, and bed sores. Help them get free treatment and pension schemes. For bedridden patients, teach families about palliative care.

lightbulb
Remember

Remove slippery floors and give good light at night to prevent falls.

emergency5. First aid and emergencies

emergencySnake bite

Keep the person calm and still. Remove rings and tight things. Keep the bitten limb still with a splint. Rush to the hospital for anti-snake venom. Do not cut, suck, burn the bite, or tie a tight tourniquet.

lightbulb
Remember

Do not waste time with faith healers.

local_fire_departmentBurns

Pour clean cool running water on the burn for 20 minutes. Remove jewellery. Cover with a clean cloth. Do not apply toothpaste, oil, or ink. Refer large or deep burns.

lightbulb
Remember

Wrap the person in a clean sheet to keep them warm.

healingBleeding and injuries

Press firmly on the bleeding wound with a clean cloth. Raise the injured part. In a road accident, do not move the neck; call 108.

lightbulb
Remember

Keep the emergency numbers 108 and 102 handy.

wb_sunnyHeat stroke and poisoning

Heat stroke: move to shade, cool with wet cloth, give fluids if awake, refer. Poisoning (pesticide, kerosene): do not make the person vomit, take the container to the hospital.

lightbulb
Remember

Keep pesticides locked and away from children.

  2. All people aged 30 and above
  3. Only pregnant women
  4. Only sick people

Answer: All people aged 30 and above

CBAC is filled for everyone aged 30 years and above.

2. A person on BP medicine feels fine now. What should they do?
  1. Stop the medicine
  2. Take it only when there is headache
  3. Continue the medicine daily
  4. Take half dose
Show answer
Answer: Continue the medicine daily

BP medicines must be continued daily; feeling well means they are working.

3. An ulcer in the mouth for 3 weeks in a tobacco user may be:
  1. Normal
  2. A sign of oral cancer - refer
  3. Due to spicy food only
  4. A vitamin problem only
Show answer
Answer: A sign of oral cancer - refer

Ulcer not healing in 2 weeks needs screening for oral cancer.

4. Tele-MANAS mental health helpline number is:
  1. 108
  2. 14416
  3. 1098
  4. 102
Show answer
Answer: 14416

Tele-MANAS: 14416, free and available 24x7.

5. First aid for snake bite is:
  1. Cut and suck the wound
  2. Tie a tight rope
  3. Keep the limb still and rush to hospital
  4. Go to a faith healer
Show answer
Answer: Keep the limb still and rush to hospital

Keep calm, immobilise the limb, and reach the hospital for anti-snake venom.

6. What is the first aid for a burn?
  1. Apply toothpaste
  2. Cool running water for 20 minutes
  3. Apply oil
  4. Burst the blisters
Show answer
Answer: Cool running water for 20 minutes

Cool running water for 20 minutes, then cover with a clean cloth.
