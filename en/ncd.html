Quiz

Try these questions. Tap "Show answer" to check.

1. For whom is the CBAC form filled?
  1. All children
  2. All people aged 30 and above
  3. Only pregnant women
  4. Only sick people
Show answer
Answer: All people aged 30 and above

CBAC is filled for everyone aged 30 years and above.

2. A person on BP medicine feels fine now. What should they do?
  1. Stop the medicine
  2. Take it only when there is headache
  3. Continue the medicine daily
  4. Take half dose
Show answer
Answer: Continue the medicine daily

BP medicines must be continued daily; feeling well means they are working.

3. An ulcer in the mouth for 3 weeks in a tobacco user may be:
  1. Normal
  2. A sign of oral cancer - refer
  3. Due to spicy food only
  4. A vitamin problem only
Show answer
Answer: A sign of oral cancer - refer

Ulcer not healing in 2 weeks needs screening for oral cancer.

4. Tele-MANAS mental health helpline number is:
  1. 108
  2. 14416
  3. 1098
  4. 102
Show answer
Answer: 14416

Tele-MANAS: 14416, free and available 24x7.

5. First aid for snake bite is:
  1. Cut and suck the wound
  2. Tie a tight rope
  3. Keep the limb still and rush to hospital
  4. Go to a faith healer
Show answer
Answer: Keep the limb still and rush to hospital

Keep calm, immobilise the limb, and reach the hospital for anti-snake venom.

6. What is the first aid for a burn?
  1. Apply toothpaste
  2. Cool running water for 20 minutes
  3. Apply oil
  4. Burst the blisters
Show answer
Answer: Cool running water for 20 minutes

Cool running water for 20 minutes, then cover with a clean cloth.
