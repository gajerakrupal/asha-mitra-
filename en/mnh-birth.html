Mother & Newborn Care · 3/5

Birth preparedness and safe delivery

checklistMake a birth plan

Decide in advance: which hospital, how to reach there (108/102 number), who will go with her, money for emergency, and a blood donor from the family.

lightbulb
Remember

Write the phone numbers on the MCP card.

local_hospitalDelivery only in hospital

Every delivery should happen in a health facility with trained staff. Under JSSK, delivery, medicines, tests, food, blood and transport are free in government hospitals. Under JSY, the mother gets cash help.

lightbulb
Remember

Home delivery increases the risk of death for the mother and baby.

bedStay 48 hours

After a normal delivery, the mother and baby should stay in the hospital for at least 48 hours. Most dangers like heavy bleeding happen in the first 2 days.

lightbulb
Remember

Visit the mother at home on the day she returns.

warningDanger signs after delivery

Heavy bleeding, fever, foul-smelling discharge, severe belly pain, fits, or feeling very sad and not caring for the baby. Refer her at once.

lightbulb
Remember

More than 1 pad soaked in 5 minutes means heavy bleeding.
