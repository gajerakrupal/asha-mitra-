Eye, Ear, Mouth & Palliative Care

personPrepared by Dr. Krupal Gajera, resident doctor in Community Medicine · Last updated: 26 October 2026

Eye care, hearing, oral health and care of bedridden patients

visibility1. Eye care

elderlyCataract

Cataract makes vision cloudy, mostly in older people. The black centre of the eye may look white or grey. Cataract surgery is free at government hospitals under the National Programme for Control of Blindness and Visual Impairment.

lightbulb
Remember

Make a list of elderly people who cannot see well and help them reach the eye camp.

child_careChildren's eyes

A child who holds books very close, squints, sits close to the TV, or has watery eyes and headaches may need glasses. Refer for eye testing. A white spot in the pupil of a baby or crossed eyes also need referral.

lightbulb
Remember

Glasses for school children are given free under the school eye programme.

warningEye injury and red eye

If something enters the eye, wash it with plenty of clean water. Do not rub. Do not put kajal, oil, milk or any home remedy in the eye. Refer eye injuries, a painful red eye or sudden loss of vision at once.

lightbulb
Remember

People with diabetes should get their eyes checked every year.

hearing2. Ear and hearing care

child_friendlyHearing in babies

A baby should startle at loud sounds from birth and turn towards sounds by about 6 months. If not, refer for a hearing test. Early help lets the child learn to speak.

lightbulb
Remember

Newborn hearing screening is done under RBSK.

healingEar discharge

Pus or fluid coming from the ear needs a doctor. Do not put oil, hot water or any liquid in the ear. Keep the ear dry. Do not clean ears with sticks, pins or matchsticks.

lightbulb
Remember

Untreated ear discharge can lead to deafness.

elderlyHearing loss in adults

Elderly people who speak loudly, ask for things to be repeated or keep the TV very loud may have hearing loss. Refer them for testing; hearing aids can help. Protect ears from very loud noise.

lightbulb
Remember

Face the person and speak clearly — do not shout.

dentistry3. Oral health

clean_handsHealthy teeth

Brush twice a day — morning and before bed — with fluoride toothpaste. Rinse the mouth after eating. Eat fewer sweets and sugary drinks. For babies, clean the gums with a soft clean cloth.

lightbulb
Remember

Children should not sleep with a bottle of milk or sweet drink in the mouth.

sickSigns to refer

Refer for: tooth pain, black holes in teeth, swollen or bleeding gums, bad smell, swelling of the face, or a mouth ulcer or white or red patch that does not heal in 2 weeks.

lightbulb
Remember

Oral screening is done at Ayushman Arogya Mandir.

smoke_freeTobacco and the mouth

Gutkha, mawa, khaini, bidi and cigarettes stain teeth, damage gums and cause mouth cancer. Advise every tobacco user to quit and check the mouth of tobacco users at every visit.

lightbulb
Remember

Quit Tobacco helpline: 1800-11-2356.

bed4. Care of bedridden patients (palliative care)

bedPrevent bed sores

Change the patient's position every 2 hours. Keep the skin clean and dry. Use soft padding under the back, hips and heels. Check the skin every day for red areas. Give enough food and water.

lightbulb
Remember

A red area that does not go away is the start of a bed sore. Tell the ANM.

medicationPain and comfort

Pain can be treated. Tell the MO if the patient has pain, breathlessness, vomiting or cannot sleep. Keep the mouth clean and moist. Help with toilet needs gently and keep the patient's dignity.

lightbulb
Remember

Palliative care is for the patient and the family.

favoriteSupport the family

Caring for a bedridden person is tiring. Teach the family how to turn the patient, clean and feed. Listen to their worries. Link them to the home-based palliative care team where available.

lightbulb
Remember

Your regular visit itself is a big support.

  1. Very costly
  2. Free
  3. Only for children
  4. Not available

Answer: Free

It is free under the national blindness control programme.

2. Something has entered a person's eye. First aid is:
  1. Put kajal
  2. Rub the eye
  3. Wash with plenty of clean water
  4. Put oil
Show answer
Answer: Wash with plenty of clean water

Wash with clean water. Do not rub or put anything in the eye.

3. A child has pus coming from the ear. You should:
  1. Put warm oil
  2. Clean with a stick
  3. Keep ear dry and refer to doctor
  4. Ignore it
Show answer
Answer: Keep ear dry and refer to doctor

Keep the ear dry and refer. Nothing should be put in the ear.

4. How often should teeth be brushed?
  1. Once a week
  2. Twice a day
  3. Once a month
  4. Only when painful
Show answer
Answer: Twice a day

Brush in the morning and before bed with fluoride toothpaste.

5. To prevent bed sores, the patient's position should be changed every:
  1. 2 hours
  2. 12 hours
  3. 2 days
  4. Week
Show answer
Answer: 2 hours

Turn the patient every 2 hours and keep the skin clean and dry.
