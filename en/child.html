vaccines1. Immunization schedule

vaccinesAt birth

BCG, OPV zero dose and Hepatitis B birth dose (within 24 hours).

lightbulb
Remember

Babies born in hospital should get these before going home.

vaccinesAt 6, 10 and 14 weeks

6 weeks: OPV-1, Pentavalent-1, Rotavirus-1, fIPV-1, PCV-1. 10 weeks: OPV-2, Pentavalent-2, Rotavirus-2. 14 weeks: OPV-3, Pentavalent-3, Rotavirus-3, fIPV-2, PCV-2.

lightbulb
Remember

Check the MCP card and write the next due date.

vaccinesAt 9-12 months

MR-1 (Measles-Rubella), fIPV-3, PCV booster, JE-1 (in JE districts), and the first dose of Vitamin A.

lightbulb
Remember

A child who has completed all vaccines by 1 year is 'fully immunized'.

vaccinesAt 16-24 months and later

16-24 months: MR-2, JE-2, DPT booster-1, OPV booster, Vitamin A (2nd dose). Then Vitamin A every 6 months till 5 years. DPT booster-2 at 5-6 years. Td at 10 and 16 years.

lightbulb
Remember

Missed doses can be given later - never tell a family it is 'too late'.

groupsYour role on VHSND / session day

Make a due list, call the families a day before, bring the children to the session, and follow up those who did not come. Mild fever or pain after a vaccine is normal. Report any serious reaction to the ANM immediately.

lightbulb
Remember

Find 'zero-dose' children who never got any vaccine.

restaurant2. Child nutrition and growth

restaurantFood from 6 months

At 6 months, start soft mashed food along with breast milk: thick dal-rice, khichdi, mashed vegetables and fruits, egg. Start with 2-3 spoons and increase slowly. Continue breastfeeding till 2 years or more.

lightbulb
Remember

Food should be thick enough to stay on the spoon.

scaleMonitor growth

Weigh the child every month and mark it on the growth chart in the MCP card. If weight does not increase, or the line goes down, counsel the mother and inform the ANM and Anganwadi worker.

lightbulb
Remember

Go to the Anganwadi with the mother on weighing days.

warningSevere acute malnutrition (SAM)

Signs: very thin child with visible bones, swelling on both feet, or MUAC (arm band) in the red zone - less than 11.5 cm. Such a child needs referral to the NRC (Nutrition Rehabilitation Centre).

lightbulb
Remember

Use the MUAC tape for children aged 6 months to 5 years.

medicationVitamin A, iron and deworming

Vitamin A every 6 months from 9 months to 5 years. IFA syrup twice a week for children 6 months to 5 years (Anemia Mukt Bharat). Albendazole deworming every 6 months from 1 year (National Deworming Day).

lightbulb
Remember

Deworming tablet for 1-2 year olds is half tablet, crushed.

water_drop3. Diarrhoea: ORS and Zinc

water_dropORS for every diarrhoea

Diarrhoea means 3 or more loose stools in a day. Give ORS after every loose stool. Mix one packet in 1 litre of clean drinking water. Use the made solution within 24 hours.

lightbulb
Remember

Show the mother how to prepare ORS - let her do it herself.

medicationZinc for 14 days

Give zinc for 14 days, even if diarrhoea stops earlier. Child 2-6 months: half tablet (10 mg) daily. Child 6 months to 5 years: 1 tablet (20 mg) daily. Dissolve in breast milk or water.

lightbulb
Remember

Zinc reduces the length of diarrhoea and prevents it in the next months.

restaurantContinue feeding

Continue breastfeeding and normal food during diarrhoea. Give more fluids. Do not stop food.

lightbulb
Remember

After diarrhoea, give one extra meal daily for 2 weeks.

warningWhen to refer

Refer if the child is very sleepy, cannot drink, has sunken eyes, skin pinch goes back very slowly, blood in stool, or diarrhoea for 14 days or more.

lightbulb
Remember

Handwashing with soap and clean water prevents diarrhoea.

air4. Pneumonia in children

airCount the breathing

If a child has cough or difficulty in breathing, count breaths for 1 full minute. Fast breathing is: 60 or more (under 2 months), 50 or more (2-12 months), 40 or more (1-5 years).

lightbulb
Remember

Count when the child is calm or sleeping.

warningChest in-drawing and danger signs

If the lower chest goes inside when the child breathes in, it is severe pneumonia. Also danger: not able to drink, fits, very sleepy, or vomiting everything.

lightbulb
Remember

Refer at once. Keep the child warm and continue breastfeeding on the way.

local_hospitalTreatment and prevention

Fast breathing needs antibiotic treatment from the ANM or doctor, as per your state's guideline. Prevent pneumonia with breastfeeding, full immunization (including PCV), and a smoke-free home.

lightbulb
Remember

Cooking smoke is harmful. Keep children away from the chulha.

toys5. Home-based young child care (HBYC)

calendar_monthHBYC visits

After HBNC, visit the child at home at 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15 months. Check growth, vaccines, feeding, ORS and zinc at home, and play and communication.

lightbulb
Remember

Use the HBYC checklist.

toysPlay and talk

Children learn by playing and talking. Tell parents to talk, sing and play with the baby every day, and to respond to the baby's sounds and smiles.

lightbulb
Remember

Simple home items like bowls and spoons are good toys.

visibilityNotice delays early

If the child does not smile by 3 months, cannot sit by 9 months, or cannot walk or speak words by 18 months, refer to the RBSK team or DEIC.

lightbulb
Remember

Early help gives the best results.

  2. BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B

  4. Td

Answer: BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B

BCG, OPV zero dose and Hepatitis B birth dose.

2. When is MR-1 (Measles-Rubella) given?
  1. At birth
  2. At 6 weeks
  3. At 9-12 months
  4. At 5 years
Show answer
Answer: At 9-12 months

MR-1 at 9-12 months, MR-2 at 16-24 months.

3. For how many days is zinc given in diarrhoea?
  1. 3 days
  2. 7 days
  3. 14 days
  4. Only until diarrhoea stops
Show answer
Answer: 14 days

Zinc for 14 days, even if the diarrhoea stops earlier.

4. A 1-year-old child breathes 48 times per minute. This is:
  1. Fast breathing
  2. Normal
  3. Slow breathing
  4. Only a cold
Show answer
Answer: Fast breathing

For a child of 1-5 years, 40 or more breaths per minute is fast breathing. 48 is fast - refer.

5. MUAC less than 11.5 cm in a child means:
  1. Normal growth
  2. Severe acute malnutrition - refer to NRC
  3. Child is overweight
  4. Needs only vitamins
Show answer
Answer: Severe acute malnutrition - refer to NRC

MUAC below 11.5 cm (red zone) is SAM.

6. When should complementary food start?
  1. At 2 months
  2. At 4 months
  3. At 6 months
  4. At 1 year
Show answer
Answer: At 6 months

Only breast milk till 6 months, then start soft food with breastfeeding.
