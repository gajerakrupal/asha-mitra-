High BP and diabetes

monitor_heartSilent diseases

High BP and diabetes usually have no signs in the beginning. Over years they damage the heart, brain, kidneys and eyes, and cause heart attack and stroke. Only screening can find them early.

lightbulb
Remember

BP 140/90 or more on check-up - send to the CHO/MO.

medicationMedicines every day

Once started, BP and diabetes medicines must be taken every day, usually for life. Do not stop when feeling well. Get free medicines and monthly check-ups from the Ayushman Arogya Mandir.

lightbulb
Remember

Help patients who run out of medicines to get their refill in time.

directions_walkHealthy lifestyle

Less salt (less than 1 teaspoon a day), less oil, sugar and fried food. More vegetables and fruits. Walk or exercise 30 minutes daily. Stop tobacco and alcohol. Keep weight in control.

lightbulb
Remember

Pickle, papad and namkeen have a lot of salt.

warningEmergency signs

Sudden weakness of face, arm or leg, difficulty speaking (stroke), chest pain spreading to the arm or jaw, sweating (heart attack). Call 108 immediately.

lightbulb
Remember

In stroke, reaching the hospital in the first 4 hours saves lives.
