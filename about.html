About ASHA Shiksha

ASHA Shiksha is a free training app and website for ASHA (Accredited Social Health Activist) workers and other primary health care workers in India. It has 40 short lessons in 9 modules and 5 detailed guides, in Gujarati, Hindi and English. The topics cover mother and newborn care, child health, communicable diseases, vector-borne diseases, non-communicable diseases, adolescent health, the ASHA's role and records, eye, ear and oral care, palliative care, and healthy living.

Every lesson and guide can be read on this website. The app adds read-aloud narration, cartoon videos, picture activities, quizzes and self-learning certificates, and works offline after the first visit.

About the author

ASHA Shiksha is prepared by Dr. Krupal Gajera, a resident doctor in Community Medicine in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Community Medicine is the branch of medicine that deals with public health and primary health care, the same field in which ASHAs, ANMs and Medical Officers work every day. The project was started to help ASHAs learn and revise on their own phones, in their own language, at any time.

How lessons and guides are prepared

  • Content is based on official Indian sources: the National Health Mission's ASHA training modules, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare programme guidelines (such as home-based newborn care, the Universal Immunization Programme, Anemia Mukt Bharat and the Stop Diarrhoea Campaign) and the Mother and Child Protection (MCP) card.
  • Doses, schedules and danger signs are kept the same as in the national guidelines. Where states differ, pages ask the ASHA to follow her ANM, Medical Officer and state guidelines.
  • Text is written in simple language so that it can be read aloud and understood easily, and is checked by Dr. Krupal Gajera before it is published.
  • Every page shows a "Last updated" date. Pages are reviewed when national guidelines change, and changes are listed on the Updates page.

Corrections and suggestions

If you find a mistake or want a new topic, please write to gajerakrupal17@gmail.com or use "Send a suggestion" in the app. Corrections are made as quickly as possible.

What ASHA Shiksha is not

  • It is not an official app of the Government of India, the National Health Mission or any state government.
  • It does not replace training by your ANM, Medical Officer or ASHA facilitator.
  • It does not give medical advice for individual patients. In an emergency, call 108.

Independent and free

ASHA Shiksha is a free, independent educational project by Dr. Krupal Gajera. It is not an official app of the Government of India, the National Health Mission or any state government. The website may show a small number of Google ads to cover its running costs. Ads are clearly labelled and are never placed inside the app's lessons, videos, picture activities, quizzes or certificates. No content on this site is sponsored.
